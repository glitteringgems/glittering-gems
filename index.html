The Craft

From rough stone to finished piece

  1. Source

    We trace every stone

    Each gem arrives with a paper trail back to the mine or river it came from, checked before it ever reaches the bench.

  2. Cut

    We cut for the stone, not the trend

    A cutter studies each rough stone for weeks, choosing the facet plan that keeps the most light inside it.

  3. Set

    We set it by hand

    The final setting is filed and polished under a loupe, one piece at a time, never cast from a mould twice.
